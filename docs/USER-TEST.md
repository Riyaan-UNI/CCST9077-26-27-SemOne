# User Testing Protocol

## Purpose

Five people with no quantum physics background use the site for 10 minutes while thinking aloud. We record where they get stuck and fix the top three issues.

## Participants

- 5 people who have not taken CCST9077 or any quantum mechanics course
- Mix of ages and technical comfort levels (include at least one person who rarely uses interactive websites)
- No physics students or researchers

## Setup

1. Open the site in a fresh browser (incognito/private mode)
2. Start screen recording
3. Give the participant this instruction:

   > "This is a website about quantum information made by students. Explore it for 10 minutes. Say aloud what you are thinking, what confuses you, and what you want to do next. There are no wrong answers."

4. Do not help unless the participant is completely stuck for more than 30 seconds

## Tasks (optional prompts if participant stalls)

1. Start at the home page. What is this site about?
2. Find and try the "magic box" interactive.
3. Read one glossary term.
4. Try one lab experiment (any tab).
5. Rate one hype claim.
6. Find the team page.

## What to record

For each participant, note:

- Where they paused or looked confused
- What they clicked that did not do what they expected
- Any text they read aloud and paraphrased incorrectly
- Features they discovered accidentally (good or bad)
- Questions they asked aloud

| Question | Yes / No / Partial |

## Notes

- The site has a "Start here" tour for first-time visitors. Observe whether participants notice it and whether it helps.
- The glossary tooltips (tap-to-define) should be tested: do participants discover them naturally?
- Reduced-motion mode should be tested with at least one participant who prefers reduced motion.
